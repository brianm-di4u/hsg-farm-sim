# Batch 7: crops (five growth stages each)

The style block in README.md applies. **Each image is a horizontal strip of exactly five equal square cells, side by
side, white between and around them.** The cells show the same plant at five growth stages, left to right:
1. a seed mound in a small patch of tilled soil;
2. a sprout;
3. a young plant;
4. a mature plant;
5. ready to harvest, with the produce clearly visible.

Every stage is bottom-aligned on the same baseline and drawn at the same scale, so the plant grows taller from cell
to cell. The plants are seen from the three-quarter top-down view, and each is a single plant for one 16×16 tile.
