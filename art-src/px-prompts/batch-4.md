# Batch 4: 1955 (mid-century) and 2026 (today), with their farmhouses

The style block and building rules in README.md apply. 1955: Kodachrome turquoise and coral, motor hotels, asphalt,
sodium street lights. 2026: the restored Row in new uses (brewery, spa, cultural centre, hotel), brick pavers, LED
light (research/visual-eras.md §3, §8).
