# Batch 2: 1886 (the Golden Age of Bathhouse Row) and the 1886 farmhouse

The style block and building rules in README.md apply. Width : height covers the whole subject. The Row of 1886 is
Victorian frame and brick bathhouses along the creek; the grand hotel is a large white frame resort hotel
(research/visual-eras.md §3).

1. `bld-vicbath-1.png` (1.3 : 1): an 1886 Victorian bathhouse: two storeys of cream clapboard with dark green trim, a
   mansard roof with dormers and a small cupola, a front porch with turned posts, brackets and a railing, centred door
   and steps. (Use the reference image's own design closely.)
2. `bld-vicbath-2.png` (1.2 : 1): a different 1886 bathhouse: red brick with white stone trim, two storeys, a
   Second Empire tower over the centred entrance, arched windows, an iron cresting on the roof ridge.
3. `bld-vicbath-3.png` (1.2 : 1): a third 1886 bathhouse: ochre-yellow painted wood, a steep cross-gabled roof with
   decorative bargeboards, a wraparound veranda, a small steam vent cupola, centred double door.
4. `bld-vicbath-free.png` (1.2 : 1): the 1886 free bathhouse for the poor: plain, one storey, white-painted boards,
   a low hip roof, a modest centred door with a plain porch, a water pipe and drain at the side.
5. `bld-brickstore.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1886 two-storey brick commercial block with a pressed-metal cornice, tall arched
   upper windows, a striped canvas awning over the shop windows, a centred door.
6. `bld-house3.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1886 Queen Anne cottage: one-and-a-half storeys, painted sage green with cream trim,
   a front gable with fish-scale shingles, a small porch with spindles over the centred door.
7. `bld-depot.png` (1.4 : 1): an 1886 railroad depot: a long one-storey frame building painted tan with brown trim, a
   wide overhanging roof on carved brackets, a bay window for the station agent, a centred door, a baggage cart.
8. `bld-grandhotel.png` (1.8 : 1, ten tiles wide): an 1886 grand resort hotel: a long three-storey white frame building
   with wraparound two-level verandas, many windows, a mansard roof with corner turrets and flags, a central entrance
   pavilion with a porte-cochère over the centred door.
9. `bld-farm-brick.png` (1.25 : 1): the farm in 1886: a dogtrot log house with a frame addition, board-and-batten on the
   new part, a brick chimney, a deep front porch across the centred breezeway.
