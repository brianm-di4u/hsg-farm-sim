# Batch 1: 1807 and 1871 buildings, early farmhouses

Each item: file name, then the subject. The style block and building rules in README.md apply to all of them.
Width : height is the approximate proportion of the whole subject (roof included).

1. `bld-tent.png` (1.2 : 1): an 1807 river trader's camp, a large canvas wall tent with its front flaps tied open and a
   ridge pole. Under the awning: bundled furs, a salt barrel, stacked crocks and a trade chest. Weathered cream canvas
   with darker patches and hemp ropes to stakes.
2. `bld-tent2.png` (1.2 : 1): a smaller 1807 canvas A-frame tent with one flap open, a bedroll inside, a cooking pot and
   a tripod beside the entrance, canvas patched in two places.
3. `bld-cabin.png` (1.3 : 1): a trapper's single-pen log cabin, 1807. Hewn logs with clay chinking, a mud-and-stick
   chimney on one side, a split-shake roof, a plank door at the centre, a pelt stretched on a frame by the door.
4. `bld-hut.png` (1.1 : 1): a health seeker's brush hut, 1807: a low lean-to of poles thatched with brush and bark over
   a rock ledge, a blanket hung as a door at the centre, a small water gourd by the entrance.
5. `bld-plankbath.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 wooden bathhouse. Two storeys of unpainted grey-brown board-and-batten, a
   gentle gable roof, a one-storey shed porch across the front on plain posts, a centred door, small square windows,
   a short stovepipe chimney trailing a little steam.
6. `bld-plankbath2.png` (1.1 : 1): a second, different 1871 wooden bathhouse: a high false front with a stepped parapet
   hiding a gable roof, whitewashed boards, a wide door at the centre with a wooden step, a hitching post at one side.
7. `bld-plankstore.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 general store with a tall square false front, a shed-roof porch over a plank
   sidewalk, barrels and crates on the porch, a centred double door, two display windows. Unpainted boards with a
   painted (blank) sign board above the porch.
8. `bld-plankhotel.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 boardinghouse. Three storeys of whitewashed board-and-batten, a gabled roof,
   a two-level porch (gallery above) across the front, a centred door, many small sash windows.
9. `bld-stage.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 stage stop office and stable: a one-and-a-half-storey plank building with a wide
   centred door, a feed loft hatch in the gable, a water trough and a wagon wheel leaning by the wall.
10. `bld-drug.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 druggist's shop with a false front, a shed porch, a centred door between two
    windows lined with bottles, stoneware jugs on a bench by the door.
11. `bld-house2.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 town house, one-and-a-half storeys of board-and-batten with a steep gable facing
    front, a small centred porch with two posts, a stone chimney at one end.
12. `bld-mill.png` (1.1 : 1): an 1871 water-powered grist mill: a two-storey timber frame building on a fieldstone base,
    a big wooden waterwheel on its right side with a wooden flume, a centred door with a loading beam above it.
13. `bld-farm-cabin.png` (1.25 : 1): the farm's first house, 1807: a single-pen log cabin with a stick-and-clay chimney,
    a split-shake roof, a small porch roof over a centred door, a woodpile against one side.
14. `bld-farm-plank.png` (1.25 : 1): the farm in 1871: a dogtrot log house (two log pens joined under one roof by an open
    central breezeway, which is where the "door" is), split-rail fence stub at one corner, a stone chimney at each end.
