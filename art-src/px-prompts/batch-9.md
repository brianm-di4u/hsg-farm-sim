# Batch 9: era panoramas (title screen and era transition)

The style block in README.md applies, **except that these are full scenes with no white background**: each fills a
wide 3 : 2 frame edge to edge. All six share one composition, so the transition reads as the same valley a
generation later:
- a side view across the Hot Springs valley from the west;
- the forested Ouachita ridges along the top third;
- the town in the middle right, at the foot of a hillside where steam rises from the springs;
- the creek winding down to the bottom left;
- the Vale family farm in the left foreground.

No text, no people close up.

1. `vista-1.png` (1807): unbroken pine and oak forest, steam rising from bare grey tufa slopes, a few canvas tents and
   a log cabin by the creek, the farm a small clearing with a log cabin. Cool morning light.
2. `vista-2.png` (1871): a single dirt street of plank buildings with false fronts along the creek, wooden bathhouses,
   a water mill; the farm with a dogtrot house and split-rail fences. Soft afternoon light.
3. `vista-3.png` (1886): Bathhouse Row of Victorian frame and brick bathhouses, a large white grand hotel with turrets,
   a railroad depot with a steam train; the farm with a frame house and a barn. Golden light.
4. `vista-4.png` (1931): the rebuilt Row in cream and red-tile revival styles, a twin-tower hotel, a radio mast, a new
   lake with raw red-clay shores in the distance; the farm with a bungalow and a Model A. Bright summer light.
5. `vista-5.png` (1955): the Row plus motor hotels and a drive-in sign (blank), cars on asphalt, the lake full with
   boats; the farm with a red barn and a tractor. Warm late afternoon.
6. `vista-6.png` (2026): the restored Row with market canopies, tree-lined streets, lake homes along the shore; the
   farm with a metal-roofed farmhouse and solar panels. Evening light with lit windows.
