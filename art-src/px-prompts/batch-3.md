# Batch 3: 1931 (the open town) and the 1931 farmhouse

The style block and building rules in README.md apply. By 1931 the Row had been rebuilt (1912–23) in revival styles:
Mission/Spanish Revival, Neoclassical and Renaissance Revival bathhouses in cream stucco, limestone and red tile
roofs, with five-globe cluster street lights (research/visual-eras.md §3–4).

1. `bld-row-1.png` (1.2 : 1): a 1931 Spanish Mission Revival bathhouse: cream stucco walls, a red clay-tile roof, a
   curved Mission parapet over the centred entrance, an arcade of round arches across the front, wrought-iron lamps.
2. `bld-row-2.png` (1.2 : 1): a 1931 bathhouse with a tiled dome: yellow brick, a central dome of blue-and-gold glazed
   tile over the centred entrance, a terracotta frieze, tall arched windows.
3. `bld-row-3.png` (1.2 : 1): a 1931 Neoclassical bathhouse: white limestone, four tall columns under a pediment over
   the centred entrance, a flat roof with a balustrade, tall windows.
4. `bld-row-4.png` (1.2 : 1): a 1931 Renaissance Revival bathhouse: cream brick, a red tile hip roof with wide eaves on
   brackets, an arched loggia on the upper floor, a centred entrance with a canopy.
5. `bld-brickstore4.png` (1.1 : 1): a 1931 brick feed and seed store: two storeys, a flat parapet, a green canvas awning,
   sacks of feed on the sidewalk, a centred door.
6. `bld-club.png` (1.1 : 1): a 1931 supper club: two storeys, dark red brick and black marble at street level, an Art
   Deco stepped parapet, a marquee canopy over the centred door, a vertical blade sign frame (blank, no letters).
7. `bld-radio.png` (1.1 : 1): a 1931 radio station: a small two-storey Art Deco building in cream stucco with a stepped
   centre, a tall steel lattice radio mast rising behind it, a centred door.
8. `bld-twinhotel.png` (1.8 : 1, ten tiles wide): a 1931 twin-tower resort hotel: two tall cream towers with red tile
   caps joined by a long multi-storey wing, Spanish Revival details, a grand centred entrance with a canopy.
9. `bld-court.png` (1.8 : 1, ten tiles wide): a 1931 tourist court: a row of small white cottages with red roofs and
   carports joined in a U, a central office cottage with the door at the centre, a gravel court.
10. `bld-house4.png` (1.1 : 1): a 1931 town bungalow: one storey, a low-pitched front gable, a wide porch on tapered
    columns over brick piers, clapboard painted light grey-blue, centred door.
11. `bld-farm-bungalow.png` (1.25 : 1): the farm in 1931: a Craftsman bungalow with a deep front porch on tapered
    columns, exposed rafter tails, a brick chimney, a centred door; a small gambrel-roofed shed at one side.
