# Batch 8: terrain textures (seamless)

The style block in README.md applies, **except the background: these textures fill the whole square edge to edge**,
with no white and no border. Each one is a seamless, tileable, top-down ground texture with no single prominent
feature: an even scatter of small flat-cluster details that repeats without visible seams. The palette is limited
to 6–10 colours.

1. `tex-grass.png`: short meadow grass, mid green with small darker and lighter blade clusters.
2. `tex-forest.png`: forest floor: dark green moss, pine needles, a few small leaves.
3. `tex-path.png`: a worn dirt foot path, packed tan earth with pebbles.
4. `tex-road.png`: an 1870s rutted red-clay wagon road with wheel ruts.
5. `tex-gravel.png`: an 1880s macadam road of packed grey gravel.
6. `tex-brick.png`: a 1930s red brick street in a running bond.
7. `tex-asphalt.png`: 1950s dark grey asphalt, slightly speckled.
8. `tex-pavers.png`: modern herringbone brick pavers in warm grey and terracotta.
9. `tex-flagstone.png`: irregular flagstone paving with mortar joints.
10. `tex-sand.png`: pale lake-shore sand with small shells and pebbles.
11. `tex-clay.png`: raw red-orange Arkansas clay, freshly exposed.
12. `tex-tufa.png`: travertine tufa: cream and pale-grey porous stone, rust streaks, a few blue-green algae spots.
13. `tex-creek.png`: a shallow clear creek bed: brown and grey pebbles seen through blue-green water.
14. `tex-lake.png`: deep lake water: blue-green with small wave highlights.
15. `tex-soil.png`: tilled dry farm soil in furrows, mid brown.
16. `tex-soil-wet.png`: the same tilled soil watered, darker brown with a slight sheen.
17. `tex-snow.png`: fresh snow cover, white with pale blue shadows.
18. `tex-rock.png`: a bare rock ridge top: grey stone with cracks and a little lichen.
