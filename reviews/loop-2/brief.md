## What changed this loop (facts only)
- **Art source replaced.** Buildings, trees, props, lamps, vehicles, animals, crops and terrain textures were all regenerated by Codex as 16-bit pixel art (prompts in `art-src/px-prompts/`, one approved pilot as the style reference), instead of being converted from the painted pictures. The pack-time pixelizer then places them on the 16-px grid at their drawn size, with no smoothing for these sources.
  - 1871 buildings are weathered, unpainted board-and-batten (three of them were recoloured from an 1886 palette).
  - The 1931 Row is Mission, tiled-dome, Neoclassical and Renaissance Revival bathhouses; 2026 shows the same four restored.
  - Crop strips keep one scale across their five stages.
  - Terrain textures were packed with fewer colours, a narrower lightness range and a dithered seamless wrap.
- **Springs (1886 on):** generated stone spring boxes, the open display spring and a built soaking pool are drawn as sprites.
- **Snow:** buildings have pre-built snow-roof variants.
- **Signs:**
  - 1931 and 1955 bathhouses carry real Row names (Superior, Quapaw, Ozark, Hale) and the 2026 hotel reads "Hotel Hale".
  - 1871 and 1886 signs vary (Hot Baths, Vapor Baths, Bath House).
  - Signs use a compact 3×5 bitmap capital font on a small board over the door.
- **Characters:**
  - Eyes are placed as one crisp dark pixel column per eye, located from the full-resolution render; the blush smudges are gone.
  - The chef's toque is smaller.
  - The bonnet brim frames the face instead of covering it.
- **Tool action:** the tool is drawn a full tile long in five held poses matching the body (wind-up, top, strike, impact, recover). The soil, splash or chip burst lands on the impact frame.
- **Light:**
  - Lamp pools no longer spill onto building facades or roofs: buildings are re-darkened over their silhouettes, then their own windows and signs are lit.
  - Dusk is warmer and stronger, rain darkens the scene, and the golden hour is softer over water.
  - Water keeps its own blues (less palette tint).
- **Occlusion:** a player hidden behind a building shows as a faint x-ray copy; the building stays solid. Lamp posts in front of the player turn see-through.
- **Fences:** the pen and placed fences are pixel post-and-rail fences that join to their neighbours.
- **Ambient:** chimney smoke rises from homes and shops on cool mornings, evenings, autumn and winter days.
- **UI type:**
  - The body font is now DotGothic16 (16-px grid), drawn at whole multiples of its pixel on every device pixel ratio; Pixelify Sans's small-size 2/8, 5/S and C/O were ambiguous.
  - Small headings use Silkscreen. Era display faces appear only at title size.
- **Performance:** the pixel style caches and pre-bakes the whole map on quiet frames after a scene starts. The phone 6× proxy went from 3.3 fps (snow town) to 50–62 fps in every scene.
- **Capture changes:** R16's close-up frames every visible neighbour near the player.
